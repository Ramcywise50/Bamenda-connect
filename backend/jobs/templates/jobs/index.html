About Bamenda Connect

Connecting local talent with local employers

Our Story

Bamenda Connect was born out of a simple observation — talented people in Bamenda were struggling to find jobs, and local employers were struggling to find the right candidates. The traditional methods of job searching through word of mouth, notice boards, and newspapers were no longer sufficient for a growing city like Bamenda.

We built Bamenda Connect to bridge this gap — a platform designed specifically for the Bamenda Metropolitan Area, supporting both English and French, and integrated with local mobile money payment systems that our community already uses and trusts.

Our Mission

To empower every person in Bamenda with equal access to employment opportunities, and to help local businesses find the talent they need to grow and thrive.

Our Values
